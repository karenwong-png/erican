M Adora · Wangsa Melawati
M Adora
~ closest to campus, low density, walk to class ~
Mah Sing’s M Adora rises in two 31-storey towers over Jalan Wangsa Melawati 1, handed over ahead of schedule on a low-density 4.5-acre parcel. Thirty-five facilities crown it — an Olympic-length horizon pool, a cantilevered glass gymnasium fitted out with Technogym, and a sky bridge strung across Level 29 with the Melawati hills beyond. At 1.2 km it is the closest address to campus of the five: fifteen minutes on foot, five by car. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.
MH Platinum 2 · Setapak
MH Platinum 2
~ newest keys, courtyard living, LRT-linked ~
The newest keys of the five. MH Group and Platinum Victory arranged three blocks and 1,254 units around a central courtyard on 4.72 acres of Jalan Usahawan 6, completed in June 2025. The facility list reads like a resort brief — swimming and spa pools, sauna, yoga deck, hammock garden, sunset deck and a jogging track threaded through the landscaping. Setapak Central, AEON Alpha Angle and Wangsa Maju LRT are minutes away; campus is 3.2 km east. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.
Rica Residence · Sentul
Rica Residence
~ freehold, twin sky decks, MRT at the door ~
Rica Residence stands alone — one freehold Fajarbaru tower of 473 units climbing 39 storeys above Jalan Perhentian in Sentul, with landscaped gardens stitched in every three floors. Its two facility decks, on Level 8 and Level 33A, carry an infinity pool set against the KL skyline, a glass sky bridge, an open-air theatre, a trampoline park and an outdoor meditation garden. Sentul Barat MRT on the Putrajaya Line sits roughly 300 m from the lobby. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.
M Vertica · Taman Pertama, Cheras
M Vertica
~ the largest facilities deck in Kuala Lumpur ~
Mah Sing’s landmark on Jalan Cheras: five towers, 3,684 units, and a 4.54-acre podium that is still the largest recreational deck in Kuala Lumpur. Thirty-eight facilities sit on it — a 50-metre pool, a 4,000 sq ft gymnasium, a 400-metre jogging and cycling loop, tennis, futsal, basketball and badminton courts, a tea garden and a maze garden. The Maluri MRT–LRT interchange is 500 m away under cover, which makes it the best-connected address of the five. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive, with Master, Double and Single types available.
Unio Residence · Kepong
Unio Residence
~ green-certified, rooftop pool, sky-deck living ~
Unio Residence closes the set — two 36-storey Aikbee towers on Jalan Kepong, completed in 2021 and built to Green Building Index standards. More than fifty facilities are stacked through it, crowned by a 60-metre rooftop infinity pool, a standalone gymnasium with a 360-degree view, a sky yoga deck on Level 36 and a sky amphitheatre lounge. Kepong Baru MRT on the Putrajaya Line is about 400 m away. Room types and rates are being finalised.

Getting to the Malaysian Institute of Art

MIA sits at 294–299 Jalan Bandar 11 in Taman Melawati, just off the rail network — Wangsa Maju on the Kelana Jaya Line is the nearest LRT at about 2 km. Pick a residence to see how far it is from campus, which station serves it, and how long the trip usually takes.
