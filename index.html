M Vertica · Taman Pertama, Cheras
M Vertica
~ the largest facilities deck in Kuala Lumpur ~
Mah Sing’s landmark on Jalan Cheras: five towers, 3,684 units, and a 4.54-acre podium that is still the largest recreational deck in Kuala Lumpur. Thirty-eight facilities sit on it — a 50-metre pool, a 4,000 sq ft gymnasium, a 400-metre jogging and cycling loop, tennis, futsal, basketball and badminton courts, a tea garden and a maze garden. Taman Pertama MRT is at the door and the Maluri MRT–LRT interchange is 500 m away under cover, which puts the campus a single change away. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive, with Master, Double and Single types available.

Nexus Residence · Taman Pertama, Cheras
Nexus Residence
~ newest keys, two MRT stations on foot ~
The newest address of the four. MKH Berhad handed over Nexus @ Taman Pertama in 2025 — one freehold 41-storey tower of 507 units on Jalan Cheras, built as a transit-oriented development with two Kajang Line stations, Taman Pertama and Taman Midah, inside walking distance. Three whole levels are given over to facilities and planting, topped by a sky infinity pool, a sky lounge and a gymnasium with the Cheras skyline on three sides. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.

The Parc3 · Pudu Impian, Cheras
The Parc3
~ forty facilities in four zones, one 39-storey block ~
Eupe Corporation’s Parc3 stands as a single 39-storey block of 795 units on Jalan Pudu Impian, completed in 2021. Its forty-odd facilities are arranged as active, passive, leisure and healthy zones rather than one flat deck — a cascading waterfall running down the podium, a celebrity kitchen, a business centre, a herb garden, pool, sauna, gymnasium and a jogging track. Taman Mutiara on the Kajang Line is the nearest station. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.

Meta City · Seri Kembangan
Meta City
~ MRT at the door, a mall downstairs, one train to class ~
The one address outside Kuala Lumpur, and the best connected of the four. Villamas built Meta City on Jalan Atmosphere Utama 2 in Pusat Bandar Putra Permai as a whole mixed quarter — a 39-storey residential tower of SoHos and serviced apartments, 1,452 homes in all, over a lifestyle mall, a hotel and a corporate tower, with 4,500 parking bays beneath. Facilities are stacked into a Sky Club at the top: pool and pool lounge, jacuzzi, viewing deck, gymnasium, games room, reading room, basketball court and BBQ terrace. Taman Putra Permai MRT sits about 200 m away on a covered link bridge — and it is on the same Putrajaya Line that stops at the campus door, so the trip to class is one train and no transfers. Rooms are fully furnished and managed end to end by BeLive.

Getting Around

Getting to Erican College

The city campus is at Block C, Megan Avenue II, No. 12 Jalan Yap Kwan Seng — a few minutes’ walk from the Petronas Twin Towers. KLCC on the Kelana Jaya Line is the closest station; Ampang Park, a little further along Jalan Ampang, adds the Putrajaya Line and makes the campus a rail interchange. Pick a residence to see how far it is, which station serves it, and how long the trip usually takes.

Nearest station

Taman Pertama on the Kajang Line is at the door, and the Maluri MRT–LRT interchange is 550 m away under cover. Ride the Kajang Line three stops to Tun Razak Exchange, change to the Putrajaya Line and it is three more to Ampang Park, a short walk from campus — about 30 minutes all in.
